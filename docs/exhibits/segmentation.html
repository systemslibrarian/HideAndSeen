ALTERNATE REPRESENTATIONS

Same message. Different route through the standard.

Numeric, alphanumeric, and byte segments can sometimes express the same overt text. A normal reader joins them and returns only the message; a cooperating reader can treat the segmentation choice itself as a signal.

FINDING

The overt message and its QR representation are different layers.

A scanner reports the concatenated segment contents. It normally does not report which legal sequence of modes carried them. That representational choice can encode a small selection signal, but inefficient or unusual segmentation is itself an artifact an analyst can inspect.

Research basis: Koptyra & Ogiela, “Steganography in QR Codes—Information Hiding with Suboptimal Segmentation” (2024) [META], doi:10.3390/electronics13132658. This exhibit implements the same general technique with its own four transparent segment-selection rules; it is not a byte-for-byte reproduction of the paper.
