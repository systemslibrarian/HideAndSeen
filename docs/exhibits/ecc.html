Damage can be data, until it is too much damage.

FINDING

Error correction is a robustness reserve, not free capacity.

Changing a module in a distinct codeword creates a Reed-Solomon symbol error. A decoder can often restore the overt message while those errors remain within every block's correction limit. Hiding and environmental damage compete for that same finite margin.

Research basis: this generalized laboratory illustrates the family of correctable-change techniques cited in Chiang et al. (2013), Bui et al. (2014), Lin & Chen (2017), Huang et al.
