MULTI-SECRET REPRESENTATION

One object. Three different readings.

The public reader sees the overt message. A segment-aware reader recovers channel A from the representation choice. A module-aware reader recovers channel B from controlled, correctable changes.

FINDING

A single ordinary-looking object can expose different information to different readers.

The channels do not become secure merely because they require different readers. Segment overhead is visible, controlled errors consume correction margin, and comparison against a reference reveals both mechanisms.

Research basis: Koptyra & Ogiela, “Multi-secret Steganography in QR Codes” (2024) [META], doi:10.37394/23209.2024.21.49.
