Independence between checks is assumed, which is a simplification rather than a bound: it overstates combined coverage and combined alarm volume together.     THE FOUR QUESTIONS

    Ask four questions of your own method.

    WHAT THE SCANNER SEES

    An ordinary payload, exactly as in every exhibit before this one. Nothing about an adversary's knowledge of your methods is visible in the symbol.

    WHAT CHANGED UNDERNEATH

    Not the symbol — the opponent. The channel is chosen after reading which layers you inspect, so your published method becomes an input to their design.

    WHAT A SECOND READER CAN RECOVER

    Whatever the uncovered channel carries. The payload is recovered exactly as before; the only thing that changed is which mechanism was used to carry it.

    HOW AN ANALYST CAN NOTICE

    By checking the layer that was actually used, which means either checking all of them or knowing something about this opponent. A clean result from the checks you ran clears those layers and nothing else.

    FINDING

    A detection rate measured against a passive adversary is an upper bound, not an operating point.

    Every number reported on this site — the padding check that flags reliably, the 53.52% attribution accuracy, the chi-square that separates naive hiding — was measured against samples that were not built to evade it. Those measurements are honest and they are also the best case. Against someone who selects their channel after reading the method, the same detector's real-world rate is whatever the uncovered layers allow, which can be zero while the detector itself remains exactly as good as advertised.

    Assume the opponent knows the method. If the method only works while it is secret, it is not a method.

    This is Kerckhoffs's principle pointed at detection rather than encryption, and it has an uncomfortable consequence. Closing a gap means adding a check, and every added check multiplies the false alarms. Complete coverage against a rare target is not merely expensive — it can produce a queue in which almost nothing is real. The honest position is to state which layers were examined, and to claim nothing about the others.
