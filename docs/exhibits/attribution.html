WHAT CHANGED UNDERNEATH

WHAT A SECOND READER CAN RECOVER

No second message. A classifier recovers a guess at provenance: 53.52% five-way accuracy against a 20% baseline, and only segno separates reliably, at 91.2%.

HOW AN ANALYST CAN NOTICE

Train on disjoint payloads, test on held-out symbols, and read the confusion matrix rather than the headline accuracy. The off-diagonal mass is what shows the signal is too weak to identify an issuer.

FINDING
